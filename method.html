01 — Test, before it's public

Instagram's Trial Reels feature shows a new piece of content to non-followers first, before it's shared to the full account. That gives an honest read on a new format, hook, or topic without betting the whole audience's attention on a guess.

In practice, this means testing the same concept several times before it goes public, not once. One reel in this portfolio was trial-tested five to six times before release, then performed at three times the account's average after going fully public. See the DMV-Area Team case.

02 — Measure views, skip rate, and interaction rate separately

These three numbers answer different questions, and a post can win on one while losing on another:

  • Views — attention. How many people the content reached, including non-followers.
  • Skip rate — retention. Whether people actually watched or scrolled past in the first second.
  • Interaction rate — connection. Comments, shares, and saves relative to reach, not just raw count.

A hook can pull huge reach and still get skipped early. A quieter, personal post can pull a fraction of the reach and earn most of the comments. Reading these as one blended "engagement" number hides which lever actually moved.

03 — Scale what earns its place

A format that performs gets rebuilt as a repeatable series with a closing promise ("follow for the next one"), not published once and left alone. A format that performs on reach but not on interaction gets a specific next test, usually a clearer call to action or a named person on camera, rather than being repeated as-is.

04 — Write for people and for AI search

Buyers increasingly ask ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews, and Perplexity market questions before they ever open Instagram. The practical response:

  • Captions structured as direct question-answer pairs AI engines can lift and cite
  • Specific, sourced numbers in captions (skip rates, record transactions, exact figures) over generic copy
  • Named neighborhoods and agents in the text itself, not just in the visuals, so AI crawlers can map the account to a specific local market
  • Consistent contact details across every post, bio, and page, a basic trust signal for local AI search
