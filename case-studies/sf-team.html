Funnel diagnostics

Finding out which part of the funnel was actually the problem, not just reporting growth

A month where every headline number was up, and a report built to say specifically where the next problem actually was.

+90.8% content interactions in one month, growing faster than reach itself
Goal

Diagnose exactly which stage of the funnel, discovery or conversion, was the real bottleneck for next month, instead of publishing a report that just says numbers went up.

Situation

The account had a genuinely strong month by every headline number. Views, reach, and interactions were all up. That's the easy part of a report to write, and also the least useful part, because "everything is up" doesn't tell anyone which lever to pull next.

The Plan

01

Split the month into three separate signals

Views (attention), interactions (connection), and follows/messages/profile visits (commitment) were reported as three distinct numbers, not blended into one performance score, specifically so a win on one couldn't mask a stall on another.

02

Locate the actual gap

Reach and interactions were growing together, at 44.7% and 90.8% respectively, but follows sat well below both. That gap, not the headline growth, was the real finding: the account was earning attention it wasn't converting into commitment.

03

Separate what drove discovery from what drove depth

Identified which specific posts pulled in non-follower reach (77% of total reach came from non-followers) versus which posts were converting people who already knew the account, since those needed different next steps.

04

Close with eight named tests, not a summary

Every recommendation was written as a specific, testable change tied to one hypothesis and one success metric for the following month, for example: person-first hook vs. location-first hook, measured against views and interactions specifically, not vague direction like 'post more.'

Results

+90.8%content interactions in one month
+44.7%reach, growing slower than interactions
77%of reach came from non-followers
6.7average views per account reached on the top post, meaning people replayed it
A report that says numbers went up isn't a diagnosis. This one exists to end in eight specific, testable actions for next month, each with its own hypothesis and its own success metric, not a recap of what already happened.
