Personal brand

Breaking a plateau by putting the agent on camera instead of a voiceover

A real estate account stuck on staged, voiceover-led listing videos. The fix was less production, not more.

+291% overall view growth over the collaboration
Goal

Break a view-growth plateau by rebuilding the account around the agent's own presence on camera, without losing the listings that pay the bills.

Situation

The account ran on staged listing walkthroughs with a voiceover layered over the footage after the fact. Views had plateaued, and because the agent never appeared on camera, none of the content was building a face anyone would recognize.

The Plan

01

Audit what was already winning

Before changing anything, pulled three months of posts and sorted them by views, reach, and interactions separately. Listing posts held reach; anything with the agent's face on it, even briefly, held interactions. That gap was the whole strategy.

02

Replace voiceover with face-to-camera

Rebuilt the production process so the agent narrates directly to camera instead of a scripted voiceover added after filming, starting with lower-stakes content (market updates, local spots) before moving to listings.

03

Reframe listings as lifestyle-first

Kept the listings, since they pay the bills, but changed the opening seconds: lead with the neighborhood or the life around the property, then transition into the walkthrough, instead of opening on the front door.

04

Introduce and test a swipe-format market report

Built a multi-card 'swipe' format for market data specifically because it creates a curiosity gap card-to-card, and ran it head-to-head against the account's existing single-image market posts.

05

Track four numbers separately, monthly

Views, interactions, follower growth, and profile visits were reported as four distinct lines, not one blended score, specifically so a win on reach couldn't hide a stall in trust-building.

Results

+291%overall view growth
+78%follower growth, February to April
+101.8%interaction growth, March to April
+184.3%profile visit growth, December to February
The single highest-interaction post of the collaboration wasn't a listing. It was an unscripted moment with the agent's kids. The algorithm read the comments and shares on that post as a trust signal, not just a view, and pushed it accordingly, which became the case for doing less production, not more.
