Full-funnel architecture

A seven-phase content system for a team with $1B+ in career sales, built to look like it

An established offline reputation, translated into a more deliberate digital presence, phase by phase, instead of a generic luxury real estate feed.

7 distinct content phases, sequenced over months, not a single calendar
Goal

Design a complete content architecture, not a content calendar, that turns an established $1B+ track record into a digital presence that reads as expert, discreet, and considered at every stage, from first impression to closed relationship.

Situation

The team's offline reputation was already established, a legacy built over years and more than a billion dollars in transactions. Their digital presence didn't reflect that yet. A single content calendar wasn't going to fix a gap that size; it needed a sequenced system that built credibility in a deliberate order.

The Plan

01

Foundation: establish the visual and narrative baseline

Refined profile visuals, aligned bios with the team's actual voice, and opened with content that establishes legacy in the first impression, a reel on what $1B+ in transactions actually taught the team about buying in Manhattan, paired with an immediately actionable carousel.

02

Local discovery: root the account in specific neighborhoods

Shifted toward street-by-street, neighborhood-vs-neighborhood content and hyperlocal recommendations, tagging local businesses to encourage shares and collaboration, while introducing direct-to-camera walkthroughs in place of detached, overproduced listing videos.

03

Engagement depth: turn viewing into conversation

Added open-ended prompts to every caption and increased interactive Stories stickers (polls, sliders, question boxes) specifically to build a rhythm of two-way response rather than passive viewing.

04

Design & value authority: own the expert-curator position

Introduced signature listing reels built around a specific price-and-neighborhood hook, paired with short educational content on materials and resale value, positioning the team as market strategists as much as brokers.

05

Relationship depth: make the human side visible

Shifted a portion of content to relationship-driven formats, client stories told as problem-plan-result, seasonal content with permission-based client features, reinforcing a stated philosophy of staying connected long after a deal closes.

06

Thought leadership: become the fast, digestible authority

Built a recurring 'three stats in thirty seconds' weekly format and financing-focused carousels with mortgage-professional collaborators, converting attention into qualified inquiries through low-friction, specific CTAs.

07

Network expansion: extend credibility outward

Closed with agent-to-agent collaboration content and macro-level, data-backed positioning pieces aimed at the investor and global-wealth audience, reinforcing the team's reach beyond a single market.

Results

7sequenced content phases, not a single calendar
$1B+in career transaction volume the system was built to reflect
Bi-weeklypaid boosts planned on 4-5 top-performing pieces to guide spend with organic data
1unified narrative carried across every phase, from first impression to closed relationship
The team didn't need more content, they needed a sequence. Starting with legacy and authority before ever asking for engagement, then building relationship depth only after credibility was established, is the difference between a content calendar and an actual system.
