Audit & personas

A 12-month content audit, rebuilt around three real buyer types

A South Florida real estate account audited across a full year and rebuilt around who was actually watching.

12 months audited, rebuilt around 3 buyer personas
Goal

Turn a year of undifferentiated posting into a content system built around three real buyer types, and account for the growing share of buyers who search with AI before they ever open Instagram.

Situation

The account had steady output, but no one had gone back through a full year of posts to ask which pieces were doing genuinely different jobs. At the same time, buyers were starting to ask ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews market questions before ever opening Instagram, and nothing about the account's captions was written with that shift in mind.

The Plan

01

Audit twelve months, three ways

Reviewed 26+ posts and reels across a full year, ranking every piece by views, reach, and interaction rate as three separate lists rather than one blended score, since the account's two best-performing posts turned out to be winning for opposite reasons.

02

Name three real buyer personas from actual behavior

Rather than assuming a generic 'luxury buyer,' built three specific personas from what the audience was actually engaging with: a relocator leaving an entire life behind, a longtime local homeowner deciding whether to finally sell, and an out-of-state investor comparing agents against the last record deal they heard about.

03

Map each persona to the question they're actually asking

For each persona, defined the literal question in their head ('Am I pricing this right, and is now the time?', 'Is this the right decision, in the right neighborhood?', 'Can this team move fast and get me access others can't?') and matched it to a specific content format, not a generic post type.

04

Build a four-pillar content system around the personas

Organized future content into four pillars, each with a distinct job: Authority & Market Intelligence (named agent spotlights, reduce decision paralysis with data), Social Proof & Record Moments (record-sale announcements, named wins), Personal Authority & Belonging (honest quotes, one clear CTA, turning cold reach into warm relationships), and Lifestyle & Aspiration (cinematic short reels, curiosity-gap hooks for top-of-funnel reach).

05

Build the AEO layer on top

Rewrote caption structure as direct question-answer pairs AI engines can lift and cite, standardized contact details identically across every post and bio, and started naming specific neighborhoods and agents in the text itself, not just the visuals, so AI crawlers can map the account to a specific local market.

06

Hand off a dated 30-day rollout

Closed with a week-by-week execution plan: a first utility 'save this' reel in week one, a named-agent spotlight with a direct-question CTA in week two, and the first batch of AEO FAQ carousels ready to syndicate by week four.

Results

2.5KInstagram followers
26+posts & reels audited across 12 months
15shares on the single most-shared post of the year
3named personas mapped to specific content
Two posts won for opposite reasons in the same account: a record-breaking transaction post read as news and got shared 15 times, more than anything else that year, while a quieter, personal agent-spotlight post read as trustworthy and earned the deepest engagement instead. Knowing which lever a post is supposed to pull mattered more than chasing one universal format, and it's the reason the account now has three personas instead of one generic buyer.
