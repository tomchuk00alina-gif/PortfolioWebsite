Self-initiated

Starting a channel with nothing, to find out what the content alone could do

Every other account in this portfolio came with some starting advantage. This one was built specifically to remove all of them.

79,030 views in the first 14 days, from zero
Goal

Find out how much of the growth comes from the content itself, the hooks, the thumbnails, the retention, when there's no existing audience, brand, or team to lean on.

Situation

Every client account in this portfolio starts with something: an existing audience, a recognizable agent, or an established community. This project removed all of that on purpose, a brand-new channel, an unfamiliar genre (oddly-satisfying and ASMR-style short videos), and zero subscribers on day one.

The Plan

01

Pick a genre with no personal-brand component

Chose ASMR / oddly-satisfying short-form video specifically because it has no face, no voice, and no existing relationship to lean on. Any growth would have to come from the content mechanics alone.

02

Publish a batch, not a flagship

Instead of investing in one polished video, published a batch of short videos in the same sitting, each testing a different hook style or object/texture combination, to get comparative data fast.

03

Watch thumbnail CTR and early retention first

Subscriber count moves last and lags badly on a new channel, so the working metrics were thumbnail impressions, click-through rate, and how far into each video people actually watched, both of which respond within hours.

04

Iterate on what the data showed, same day

Because YouTube's feedback loop on a new channel is fast and public, adjusted thumbnails and posting times within the same testing window rather than waiting for a weekly review cycle.

Results

79,030total views in the first 14 days
123,307thumbnail impressions
1.77%average thumbnail click-through rate
19subscribers gained with zero starting audience
The best single video pulled over 16,000 views entirely on its own, with no subscriber base pushing it and no algorithm favor from an established channel. Hook, thumbnail, and retention did that on their own, not who was already following before the first upload.
