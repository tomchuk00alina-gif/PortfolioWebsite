Outside real estate

Running four parallel accounts on the same test cycle, in a niche with zero overlap to real estate

Four astrology and entertainment communities on a different platform entirely, no personal brand, no listings, no agent.

+6,082 daily actives from a single well-timed podcast episode
Goal

Grow daily and monthly actives across four parallel entertainment communities using a disciplined monthly test cycle, and tell real account growth apart from engagement that looks good but means nothing operationally.

Situation

Four zodiac-themed entertainment communities needed steady growth in reach and monthly actives. There was no single hero account and no personal brand to lean on, just four near-identical communities that made it possible to isolate which format changes actually moved daily and monthly active users versus which ones just produced good-looking engagement numbers.

The Plan

01

Run the same monthly test cycle across all four accounts at once

Every month, tested the same set of variables in parallel across all four communities: avatar images and change timing, story-mechanic formats, podcast release timing, and live-stream slot timing, then compared results across accounts rather than trusting a single data point.

02

Track daily/monthly actives separately from surface engagement

Vote counts, story views, and poll participation were logged, but never treated as success on their own. Daily active users (DAU) and monthly active users (MAU) were the numbers that decided whether a format stayed or got cut.

03

Identify and double down on the strongest recurring format

Multi-part content collections and an 'advice of the day' interactive story format were the two most consistent DAU drivers, month after month, across all four accounts, so production time shifted toward more of both.

04

Cut formats that looked good and did nothing

One interactive story series pulled 24,932 votes in a single run, an enormous engagement number, and moved daily actives by zero. It was cut rather than repeated, freeing the production slot for a format that had already proven it moved the real number.

Results

+6,082daily actives from one well-timed podcast episode
+1,047 to +1,943daily actives from the strongest recurring story format, month to month
24,932votes on one interactive series that contributed zero to daily actives
6 monthsof logged monthly retrospectives across all four communities
The clearest finding wasn't a growth tactic, it was a warning built from a real number: a format can pull tens of thousands of votes and move the account's actual health by nothing. Running four accounts in parallel, on a niche with no overlap to real estate at all, made that gap impossible to miss and easy to prove.
