Method

I don't build content calendars first. I build a feedback loop.

The steps below run the same way whether the account is a real estate team or a channel with zero followers.

01

Test

Put a new format in front of a smaller audience first. If the idea doesn't hold attention there, there is little reason to push it harder.

02

Read

Look beyond views. Retention, skips, interactions, saves, shares and follows answer different questions — and I don't treat them as the same signal.

03

Build

The formats that prove themselves become repeatable content systems, not one-off posts.

04

Expand

Once a format works, I adapt it across topics, audiences and platforms — including search-driven content where relevant.
